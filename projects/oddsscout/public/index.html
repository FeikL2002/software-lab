Deploy the full project through Git or Netlify CLI. In Netlify’s environment variables, add ODDS_API_KEY with your key from The Odds API, include the Functions scope and your preview context, and create a new preview deploy.

KNOW WHAT YOU’RE COMPARING

The numbers, explained.

What “best” actually means

The largest payout among fresh, selected-book quotes for an identical game, market, outcome, and line. It is not a best bet, guaranteed winner, or prediction of profitability.

Same line. Same comparison.

A team at +3.5 is not the same bet as +4.5. Over 44.5 is not Over 45.5. The app lists them separately. Two-way and three-way moneylines are also kept separate. Book-specific settlement and overtime rules still need checking.

Profit versus total return

A hypothetical $25 stake at +140 earns $35 profit if it wins, for $60 returned including the original stake. A $25 stake at −125 earns $20 profit, for $45 total. Losing a bet can lose the entire stake.

What counts as live?

A game is labeled live only when the scores feed reports a non-completed event with recent score information. A scheduled start time alone is not proof. Missing or old status goes under Unconfirmed. Feed latency and delayed or suspended games can still affect accuracy.

Freshness and coverage

Quotes older than three minutes, missing timestamps, and pre-game quotes on started fixtures are excluded from “best” rankings. Snapshot and book-update times are displayed. All leagues loads every active event league in small batches, not the limited cross-sport shortcut. No app-imposed future-date cutoff is used. All dates shows all returned fixtures; Today, Tomorrow, and Next 7 days use your device’s local time. A partial scan is labeled, including failed leagues. Futures use the provider catalog when selected; game cards are for individual fixtures. Extended markets load in Game Center on demand. Missing coverage is not proof that no game exists.

My Bets & private accounts

The Save button saves a private idea; a separate thumbs-up contributes to community popularity; it is not a placed bet. Only record cash wagers you already placed outside OddsScout, using the accepted odds and stake on your receipt. Results and returned amounts are self-reported, not automatically verified or settled. Guest records stay in this browser; anyone sharing the browser profile can access them. Signed-in records are private to your OddsScout account. Demo records never sync to your real account. Community likes count signed-in users independently of private saves and statistical analysis. Popularity never changes a model probability. No sportsbook credentials, deposits or withdrawals are collected.

Netlify Identity verifies your account and manages session cookies. Cloud bet records are not copied into browser storage. Sign out on shared devices. Use Export CSV to take a copy, and Delete to remove a record. Contact the site owner for account or data deletion requests. Community chat is visible to other signed-in members; public display names appear with messages. All Winnings includes shared totals only after a member explicitly opts in.

Your data

Watchlist and display preferences stay in this browser. The entry code is verified by the server and is never stored in browser storage. A signed HttpOnly cookie keeps the entry session for up to eight hours. In live mode, the server contacts The Odds API. When AI is enabled, your question and the selected odds snapshot go to OpenAI; this app requests no response storage. Hosting and providers may retain operational logs. The recent-form server contacts BALLDONTLIE only when requested. Player Stats loads completed-game results from MLB StatsAPI or ESPN publisher data when you ask for them. Missing games and statistics are shown as unavailable.

ODDSSCOUT RESEARCH

Build with evidence.
Not with hype.

Rules-based research · not a prediction model

Explore upcoming moneylines using recent scores and market prices. “Best” here means highest research rank or estimated payout—not a proven winner. Bets are placed only on the sportsbook’s website.

On demand only. No continuous stats polling. Scans up to eight loaded upcoming games within seven days.

How these ideas are ranked

Both teams need at least three completed games and a result in the last 21 days. Results are limited to 120 days. Recent win rates are smoothed toward 50% before ranking. Home/away splits use only the displayed sample; they are not full-season records. One higher-ranked outcome per game is considered.

No opponent-strength adjustment, injury confirmation, starting lineup, weather model, backtested edge, or calibrated win chance is included. No in-play, player-prop, same-game, spread, total, or three-way parlay suggestions are made. The ordinary odds board still supports its existing markets.
